Admissions

Everything you need to apply for the Fall 2027 intake.

Admissions overview

We look for motivated students with strong mathematics and science preparation and a genuine interest in building things. Applications are reviewed holistically: grades, a short personal statement and any project work you wish to share.

Fall intake applications close on 15 March 2027. Early applicants receive decisions within three weeks.

Start your registration

How to apply

  1. Register your interest

    Complete the online registration form with your details and preferred program.

  2. Submit documents

    Upload transcripts, ID and a short personal statement through your applicant portal.

  3. Attend an interview

    Short online or on-campus conversation with a faculty member.

  4. Receive your decision

    Offers are sent by email with enrolment and scholarship details.

Admissions FAQ

What are the entry requirements?

A secondary school diploma with strong results in mathematics and physics. International applicants should provide proof of English proficiency.

Can I apply for a scholarship?

Yes. Tick the scholarship interest option on the registration form and the admissions team will share the available awards and deadlines.

Is part-time or online study available?

Selected programs offer a part-time or hybrid study mode. Choose your preferred mode on the registration form.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Absolutely. Join an Open Day or contact us to arrange a private tour of the labs.
