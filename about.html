About & Programs

Practical, project-based engineering education since 1998.

Who we are

Engineering College is a teaching-focused institution dedicated to preparing graduates who can design, build and maintain the systems society depends on. Our small classes, close faculty mentoring and strong industry ties make theory immediately useful.

Every student completes team design projects each year, culminating in a sponsored capstone and a professional internship before graduation.

Our values

  • Curiosity and rigor in every lab
  • Responsible, sustainable design
  • Collaboration across disciplines
  • Inclusive learning for every student
